Title
After School Stella — Between a Lie and a Wish

Short description
A school romance about unspoken feelings and wishes, with Japanese and English voices.

Description
Some feelings hide behind a smile. Some wishes are hard to say aloud.
Spend your after-school days with Sakura, Kotoha and Mahiru in a school romance visual novel.

Get to know the three heroines through everyday conversations and school-festival preparations. Your choices lead the story toward different endings.

- Japanese and English text and dialogue voices. The protagonist is unvoiced.
- An opening movie with localized text and title logo in each language.
- Character routes, multiple endings and bonus content to discover after completing the story.
- Original music, ending songs, a CG gallery and an unlockable Sound Mode.
- Save/load, a dialogue log with voice replay, auto-advance and read-text skipping.
- Free to play in your browser.

Controls
Click, tap, Enter or Space to advance.
Use the on-screen controls for Save, Load, Log, Auto and Skip.
Change the text/voice language, volume and reading settings in CONFIG.
On mobile, play in landscape orientation.
